Spans, treatment levels and bracing must be confirmed against NZS 3604:2011 Timber-framed buildings (or a producer statement from a Licensed Building Practitioner / engineer) before you build. Sheds under 10 m² used for ancillary storage may be exempt from building consent under Schedule 1 of the Building Act 2004 — check current thresholds and any district plan rules with your local council before starting, as exemptions don't remove the requirement to meet the Building Code.
